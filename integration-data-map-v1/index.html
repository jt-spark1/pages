Reading this map

Salesforce is the substrate. The navy band running through the middle is not "another system" - it's the record layer everything else writes to. SF-native automation (in red italics within the band) governs progression: validation rules guard create, assignment rules + lifecycle stamps drive routing, LeadConvertSettings + the OCR Sync flow handle conversion stamping.

HubSpot is the spine: active in 7 of 8 stages, owns lifecycle stage, source attribution, and the majority of activity records on Lead. Other above-the-line systems (Luma, Clay) are point integrations that fan in at specific stages.

Stage 6 fragmentation: a single sales motion produces records in at least four different stores - HubSpot Tasks, Dialpad Call_Log + Task, EAC separate store, Sendoso writeback, plus a Fathom recording that never reaches SF. Cross-channel reporting requires stitching by recipient email + timestamp. This is the structural argument for consolidation.

Clay is manual / batch: a discrete human-triggered CSV export and bulk-upload, not a live integration. Routing gotcha: SF-authoritative fields like Account.Agency_NPN__c only reach Salesforce if the CSV is pushed to SF directly - a HubSpot-side push leaves the value stuck in HubSpot.

Fathom is adjacent: reps use it for meeting summaries but neither SF nor HubSpot know it exists. A meeting can happen without producing any record beyond whatever calendar Event EAC captures (and that has no link to the Fathom recording).
