Opportunity Stage Workflow Audit

Spark Advisors GTM Systems · sc-39885 · May 28, 2026 · No changes deployed — audit only

Bottom line. The stage gates are working and the required fields are being filled. Measured on deals actually worked in the past month, required-field completion runs 76–95% at every stage — including all nine fields on the "clunky" Negotiate gate (filled on 10 of 11 deals). The fields also match what Diana's documented process says belongs at each stage. The friction reps feel is the volume of fields at once, not wrong or missing requirements. The genuine gaps are structural: a misconfigured "Develop" stage, a documented "Announcing & Onboarding" stage that doesn't exist, and several documented checkpoints that aren't captured anywhere.

Key findings

  • Gates work on active deals. Among deals whose stage changed in the past month, required fields are filled 76–95% across all stages. (The scary "5–7% filled" all-time number was stale history — the gates only shipped May 10, so older deals never faced them.)
  • Gates match the documentation. For every stage and field that exists, Salesforce requires what the process deck says belongs at that stage. Nothing is required in the wrong place.
  • The friction is UX, not the rules. Negotiate surfaces nine required fields at one save. It's worse when a rep skips stages — jumping ahead triggers every earlier stage's requirements at the same time.
  • Two stage-model gaps. "Develop" is switched on but parked at the bottom of the list with zero deals and no gate (the deck wants it as the first stage). "Announcing & Onboarding" is in the deck but doesn't exist in Salesforce.
  • The documented process is richer than what's enforced. Several documented checkpoints (mutual close plan agreed, path to signature, non-AP decision makers, releases granted) have no field, so they're never captured.

Recommendations

  • Keep the field requirements. The data says they're right and reps are meeting them. Reduce the Negotiate friction through layout grouping, clearer error messages, and coaching reps to advance one stage at a time — not by removing fields. Sign-off: Chris / RevOps
  • Fix the "Develop" stage — move it to the front of the pipeline (as the deck intends) or turn it off. Today it's the worst of both. Diana + Jonathan
  • Decide "Announcing & Onboarding" — make it a real post-close stage, or keep it in onboarding tooling and drop it from the sales-stage deck. Diana + CS
  • Decide on the unenforced documented checkpoints (mutual close plan, path to signature, etc.) — turn each into a field, a checklist item, or drop it. Diana + Chris
  • Populate Agency Type upstream so the Call Center / Hybrid requirements actually fire for those agencies org-wide. Jonathan
  • Clean up the ~58 deals stuck on retired stages and triage the large backlog of untouched Discovery deals. Jonathan
  • Ratify one canonical stage model and align the deck, the user guide, and Salesforce so they all agree. Diana + Jonathan
Source of truth: Salesforce production (sparkprod), queried May 28, 2026; Spark Sales Process deck + SFDC user guide. Population measured on the recent-activity cohort (stage changed since Apr 28, 2026). No metadata was changed — this is an audit with recommendations pending sign-off.
