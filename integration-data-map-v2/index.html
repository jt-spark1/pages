Salesforce is the substrate. The eight stages below are SF record states - the Lead is born in SF, accumulates fields in SF, and is converted in SF. SF-native automation (validation rules, assignment rules, LeadConvertSettings, the OCR Source Field Sync flow) governs progression between stages. Seven external systems read from or write to that substrate: above the line sourcing & data-push (HubSpot, Luma, Clay); below the line activity capture & rep tools (Dialpad, EAC, Sendoso, Fathom). The structural reporting gap is at Stage 6, where a single sales motion writes records into at least four different stores.
